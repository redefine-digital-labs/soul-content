The nine Content sources do not depend on these three modules and can build independently without loosening interfaces.

The overall candidate manifest still links Standard and Foundation; Walrus/Sui retain their original pinned git rev. Generic storage/document entry points may operate without Soul; this does not mean the entire Content package has eliminated Standard/Foundation dependencies. Extracting smaller adapter packages requires new actual source/ABI evidence.
